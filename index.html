Our Story

Founded in 2010 in the heart of Dhaka, The Golden Spoon began as a small family kitchen with a grand vision: to bring authentic Bengali flavors to the world stage with a modern twist.


Our founder, Chef Rahman Ahmed, traveled across Bangladesh—from the hills of Sylhet to the coastal kitchens of Cox's Bazar—collecting recipes that had been passed down for generations. Each dish on our menu tells a story of tradition, innovation, and the warmth of Bengali hospitality.


Today, we stand as a beacon of fine dining, where every ingredient is sourced from local farmers, every spice is ground fresh, and every guest is treated as family.
